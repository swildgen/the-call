The dance

December 2-6, 2026, The 360 Emergence will gather at The Thyme, 2.5 hours north of New York City, for five immersive days of movement, dance, embodied inquiry, music, meaningful conversations and time on the land.

The Call is designed to strengthen the body, sharpen perception, deepen presence + expand what is possible in how we meet ourselves, each other, and our lives.

The Thyme is 85 acres on the line between Connecticut and Massachusetts. The house is alcohol-free.

You don't need any 360 background. People already in the apprenticeship or facilitator training will be there too. Same room, same dance.

Teachers

Amber Ryan

Leads the retreat · 360 co-founder

The Call is led and curated by Amber. She's a dancer, teacher, and DJ, with decades in transformative dance and a background in theatre and voice.

Allyssa Rivera

Senior teacher · A-li-sha · she/her/ella

Allyssa is a senior 360 teacher and a dance/movement therapist. She trained at Sarah Lawrence and NYU, and she now teaches graduate students at Sarah Lawrence.

Louisa Heckett

Music

Louisa is a DJ and movement teacher who splits her time between Ireland, Spain, and Costa Rica. She started on vinyl in the nineties and still builds long sets that can actually move a room.

Rachel Jordana

Music

Rachel is a licensed psychologist, 5Rhythms teacher, and DJ. She's been offering conscious dance since 2010, in rooms that range from clinics to festivals.
